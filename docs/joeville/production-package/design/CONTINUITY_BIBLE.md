# Joeville Continuity Bible

## Locked geography for design development

- Mountains and dominant Flatiron analogue: west.
- Open valley and town entrance: east.
- Joeville Creek: west/northwest to east/southeast through the town.
- Downtown sits north of the central creek crossing; Wellness/Creek sets sit east/southeast.
- Grace Meadow is uphill southwest/west of downtown.
- Castle Grace is on the southwest western ridge above town.
- Joeville Falls is a separate canyon excursion; it is never visible from downtown.

The coordinate/node/route authority is `MAP_SPEC.md`; the illustrated overview is `../maps/JOEVILLE_PRODUCTION_MAP.svg`.

## Visual locks

- Scenic edges are softer/lighter than character outlines.
- Palette: sandstone, sage/ponderosa, warm brick, creek blue, sun gold, dusk indigo; rainbow accent is rare and motivated.
- Castle retains one approved tower count, roof silhouette, entrance, terrace and amphitheater relationship after lock.
- Storefront order, entry side, window view, counter/service geometry and exit locations never change between angles.
- Final signage comes from separate controlled graphics, never generated pixels.

## Light, season and weather

- Morning light arrives from east; west-facing mountain form is readable.
- Golden-hour packages record exact sun bearing and shadow direction per camera.
- Continuous scenes keep leaf state, snow coverage, wetness, creek level, practical lights and sky direction consistent.
- Spring permits wet ground, budding foliage and late-snow alternates; summer separates lush creek shade from drier uplands.

## Editorial rules

- Travel under 5 minutes may cut on matched exit/arrival.
- Travel 6–15 minutes needs a connective cue when geography matters.
- Travel over 15 minutes or any mountain/falls trip needs time/ascent transition.
- Westbound/ascent defaults screen-right to left; eastbound/descent defaults left to right after establishing geography.
- Plates may not be mirrored; mirroring reverses creek flow, sunlight, doors and character asymmetry.

## Set-dressing continuity

Every shot records: furniture layout version, interactive props, food/drink state, menu/poster version, doors/windows open state, practical lights, weather residue, vegetation season, and reset photograph/diagram. Interactive props use stable IDs and remain separate from clean plates.

## Rejection checklist

Reject any asset that reverses creek flow, moves mountains east, shows Falls from town, changes a window wall, changes Castle silhouette, blocks an approved path, loses a required exit, drifts character scale, introduces unapproved marks, or changes season/weather within continuous time.
