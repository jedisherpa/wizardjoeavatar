This is production-planning guidance, not legal advice.

## Graphic rules

- All final words, menus, prices, labels, posters and maps are controlled vector/graphics passes.
- Generated lettering is prohibited in masters.
- No real logos, product packaging, employee uniforms or branded color blocking without written approval.
- Event flyers and menus are episode props with continuity IDs and expiration/version dates.
