# Castle Grace Canon Lock v0.2

**Authority:** five user-supplied image references received 2026-07-13.  
**Use:** canonical visual reference for Castle Grace; the generated voxel plates are design translations, not evidence of undocumented real architecture.

## Locked exterior identity

- Warm honey/sandstone masonry with irregular rectangular blocks.
- Dark brown timber roof edges, deep eaves and mixed gable/low-roof masses.
- Blue-green glazing in dark frames, including a large triangular gable window.
- Stepped multi-level terraces with black iron rails and stone stairs.
- A monumental mature pine intertwined with the upper porch and dominating its silhouette.
- Forested foothill setting, steep drop-offs, planted retaining beds and terracotta planters.
- Timber porch decks with dark metal rocking chairs and pale cushions.

## Locked interior identity

- Sandstone fireplace wall and masonry perimeter walls.
- Dark exposed ceiling beams and warm chandelier/practical lighting.
- Red upholstered seating, substantial dark leather sofa, wood coffee table and patterned cream rug.
- French doors to a terrace and abundant planted greenery.
- Art and screen content are replaceable controlled graphics; recognizable third-party characters are not canonical.

## Continuity rules

1. The giant pine, gable glazing, sandstone, rail language and terrace stepping must survive every angle.
2. Castle production plates remain empty. The two people in source reference 01 are reference-only and were removed in the voxel plate.
3. Camera reverses must preserve door/window placement and the downslope forest orientation.
4. Generated extensions such as kitchen, library, council room, meditation space or amphitheater are explicitly fictional until a plan is approved.
5. The former church-like Joeville Castle study remains rejected and is superseded for silhouette/material direction by these references.
