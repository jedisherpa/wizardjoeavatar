   order. Every pose is independent; no duplicate, mirror, warp, or padding.
4. **Pixel integrity.** Prove complete silhouettes, outfit coverage,
   transparent edges, canonical margins, baseline `1185`, and zero RGB under
   zero alpha.
5. **Motion integrity.** Prove 48 unique RGBA graphs, support/contact
   continuity, continuous root and hair arcs, no foot skating, no root jitter,

   artifact, index, contract, manifest, and contact-sheet hashes.

   ordered sequence beside approved HD Wizard Joe.
8. **Runtime admission.** Explicit product approval must bind the exact
   artifact, index, contract, manifest, and evidence hashes before
   `runtime_admitted` may become `true`.

## Accountability Rules

- A slot changes from `pending_art` only when its own authored full-size image
  and provenance receipt exist.
- Group approval never promotes another group or any missing member.
- Visual approval is recorded against exact file and raw RGBA hashes.
- Identity anchors and motion slots remain review-only until the final gate.
- Rejected art remains hash-visible and cannot silently replace an accepted
  frame.
- A rebuild mismatch reopens gates 4-7.
- Any identity, garment, silhouette, contact, root, hair, or handoff defect
  returns the affected slot to a non-approved state.
- Runtime registries and production character manifests are outside this
  planning package and remain unchanged.
