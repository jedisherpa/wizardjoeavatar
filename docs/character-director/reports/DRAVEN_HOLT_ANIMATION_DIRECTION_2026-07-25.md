## Performance Identity

Draven reads as a grounded site foreman. His acting is economical, attentive,
and operational rather than theatrical. The clipboard is a continuous
character prop: his left hand owns exactly one clipboard and its pencil while
his free right hand performs every conversational and safety gesture.

The authored work must preserve the black flat cap, black suit, orange shirt
and tie line, bright-green rectangular glasses, beard, black shoes, voxel
construction, and the same four checklist marks. The clipboard must not change
hands, disappear, duplicate, become a shield, or become a pointing implement.

## G7 Foreman Status Exchange

Intent: `receive -> grant floor -> listen -> probe -> assign -> confirm`

| Pose | Beat | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `037` | Receive | Three-quarter attention, level chin, composed shoulders. |
| `038` | Grant floor | Low open right palm, close elbow, slight torso turn. |
| `039` | Listen | Small head-and-chest incline; right hand retracts quietly. |
| `040` | Probe | Restrained skepticism and a compact palm-up question. |
| `041` | Assign | One contained edge-led placement toward the work area. |
| `042` | Confirm | Small nod and low open palm requesting acknowledgment. |

The six-frame sequence runs at 8 fps and loops. Poses `039` and `041` are hold
frames. Poses `037`, `039`, and `042` are safe interruption markers. Entry is
from front or three-quarter idle; exit is to either idle or the G8 safety
sequence.

## G8 Site Safety Intervention

Intent: `detect -> stop -> contain -> reroute -> verify -> release`

| Pose | Beat | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `043` | Detect | Eyes and chin acquire the hazard; shoulders brace. |
| `044` | Stop | Clear vertical right stop palm with a soft elbow. |
| `045` | Contain | Torso turns toward danger while the arm blocks the lane. |
| `046` | Reroute | Low whole-hand direction toward the safe route. |
| `047` | Verify | Hand retracts as gaze checks the hazard and crew. |
| `048` | Release | Restrained nod and low palm-down proceed signal. |

The six-frame sequence runs at 8 fps and holds its final frame. Poses `044`,
`046`, and `048` are hold frames. Poses `043`, `044`, `046`, and `048` are safe
interruption markers. Entry is from idle or G7; exit is to front idle or a
profile walk after pose `048`.
