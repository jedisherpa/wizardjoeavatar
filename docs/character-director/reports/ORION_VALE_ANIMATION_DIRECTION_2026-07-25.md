## Supplied Vocabulary

The supplied 36 poses cover profile idle, walking, running, compression,
launch, airborne action, landing, turn, frontal and rear settles, toward-camera
movement, and one raised-hand wave. They do not provide enough conversational
acting or governed body language.

## Acceptance Rules

1. Preserve Orion's orange block curls, beard, square glasses, dark-brown coat,
   oatmeal shirt, burgundy trousers, and dark boots.
2. Preserve crisp voxel construction and the canonical camera and scale.
3. Keep all hands, hair, coat hems, and boots uncropped.
4. Keep hand arcs continuous and prevent scale breathing or root pops.
5. Require full-size, thumbnail, adjacency, and slowed-motion review before
   product approval.
