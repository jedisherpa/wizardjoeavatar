# Rohan Slate Animation Direction

Date: 2026-07-26
Scope: authored poses `037`-`048`
Candidate state: direction approved for authoring; product approval pending

## Source Read And Performance Identity

Rohan's supplied poses `001`-`036` already cover side walk, run, jump,
anticipation and landing, front idle and walk, rear idle and walk, a wave, and
directional presentation. The missing work must therefore add grounded acting,
not more locomotion or another generic greeting.

Rohan reads as observant, self-possessed, and direct without being hard. His
performance begins with listening, reaches decisions through contained hand
shapes, and lets the body settle after each idea. He does not bounce, swagger,
lecture, or perform broad pantomime. His glasses make small head angles
especially legible, so gaze and chin direction should carry as much meaning as
the arms.

Authored poses must preserve his dark knit beanie, green rectangular glasses,
short dark beard, white short-sleeve shirt, dark-green trousers, brown shoes,
warm skin tone, voxel construction, and established body proportions. Glasses
remain level on the face; the beanie never changes shape or exposes a new
hairline.

## G7 Measured Boundary

Intent: `register -> check -> state -> define -> allow -> resolve`

This sequence gives Rohan a calm disagreement that protects a boundary while
leaving the conversation open. The emotional temperature stays low. The
strongest pose is a clear palm boundary, not an angry point or aggressive
block.

| Pose | Beat | Pose summary | Acting direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `037` | Register | Front three-quarter stance, chin turns slightly toward the speaker, hands low and quiet. | Receive the concerning statement before reacting. Keep the brow readable behind the glasses and let a small torso orientation show attention. |
| `038` | Check | One hand rises near the lower chest with a compact palm-up question; the other remains relaxed. | Ask for clarification, not permission. Keep the elbow close and the gesture small enough to feel thoughtful. |
| `039` | State | The questioning hand returns toward the sternum with fingers gathered; shoulders square gently. | State Rohan's own position. The hand-to-self relationship must read as ownership, never a pledge or dramatic chest clutch. |
| `040` | Define | One open palm faces outward at lower shoulder height with a soft elbow; the free hand stays low. | Establish the boundary clearly. The palm is calm and vertical, the body remains rooted, and the face does not become hostile. |
| `041` | Allow | The boundary palm rotates open and slightly sideways, creating visible conversational space. | Show that another option can still be discussed. Ease the shoulders and avoid turning the gesture into a wave. |
| `042` | Resolve | Both hands settle low, one palm still faintly open; chin returns level with a restrained nod. | End with composure and retained connection. The silhouette should be close enough to neutral for a clean handoff without duplicating an idle frame. |

### G7 Timing And Handoffs

- Playback: `8 fps`, `hold_last`.

- Entry handoffs: front idle `023` or `025`, front-walk settle `029`, or a

## G8 Practical Collaboration

Intent: `frame -> compare -> locate -> propose -> confirm -> commit`

This sequence gives Rohan a second, complementary mode: jointly solving a
problem with another person. It is more outward and constructive than G7, but
still economical. Spatial relationships are communicated with open hands and
gaze; no imaginary object is held or manipulated.

| Pose | Beat | Pose summary | Acting direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `043` | Frame | Both hands rise low in front of the torso, palms angled inward to define one compact area. | Establish the size of the issue. Hands describe a shared topic, not a box, device, or invisible prop. |
| `044` | Compare | Hands separate modestly to two unequal positions while the gaze checks one side and then the other. | Weigh two options without caricature. Preserve asymmetry so the pose does not read as shrugging or presenting a prize. |
| `045` | Locate | One open hand lowers toward a specific near-ground area while the other retracts to center. | Identify where the practical problem lives. Use the whole hand; never point with one finger or bend into a crouch. |
| `046` | Propose | The locating hand sweeps up into a forward, open-palm offer while the torso inclines slightly. | Offer a workable next step. The energy moves outward through the palm but the feet and root remain fixed. |
| `047` | Confirm | The offered hand holds while the other makes a small contained approval beat near the torso. | Check agreement and show confidence in the proposal. Avoid a thumbs-up, fist pump, clap, or celebratory read. |
| `048` | Commit | Hands return low with one palm angled toward the shared work area; head gives a clear, restrained nod. | Close on practical commitment: "we can do this." The final silhouette must support a clean return to idle or departure. |

### G8 Timing And Handoffs

- Playback: `9 fps`, `hold_last`.
- Intentional hold markers: `043`, `044`, `046`, `048`.
- Safe interruption markers: `043`, `044`, `046`, `047`, `048`.
- Protected transition spans: `044 -> 045 -> 046`; preserve the complete
  compare, locate, propose thought.
- Entry handoffs: front idle `023` or `025`, G7 pose `042`, or a completed
  front-walk settle.
- Exit handoffs: front idle `023` or `025`, profile walk after the `048` hold,
  or G7 pose `037` when new information reopens the discussion.

## Root, Support, And Contact Policy

All twelve poses use a fixed root. Both shoes remain planted on canonical
baseline `1185`, with the established shoe size and spacing preserved. Weight
may transfer subtly inside the footprint through knee, hip, and shoulder
offsets, but neither shoe may slide, lift, cross, rotate into a new facing, or
imply a step.

The center of mass remains between the feet. Torso inclines are small and must
be counterbalanced through the hips rather than root translation. Arms must
remain connected at the shoulders, hands must not float or penetrate the torso,
and all gestures must preserve clear negative space around the white shirt.

## Prop Rule

Rohan carries no prop in either sequence. Do not invent a phone, clipboard,
book, tool, table, screen, diagram, or invisible handheld object. G8 hands
describe ideas and locations only; their spacing must never imply that a
missing prop should exist between them.

## Forbidden Defects

Reject any frame with:

- a duplicate or near-duplicate of source poses `001`-`036`;
- a wave, salute, dance, run, stride, crouch, jump, landing, turn, or airborne
  silhouette;
- an accusatory index-finger point, clenched threat, crossed arms, fist pump,
  shrug, clap, or exaggerated pleading;
- hostile facial acting, a broad grin during the boundary, or gaze that loses
  the conversational partner;
- changed, crooked, obscured, or floating glasses;
- altered beanie shape, beard shape, shirt length, trouser color, shoe design,
  body scale, or voxel density;
- cropped beanie, hands, elbows, shoes, or any broken exterior silhouette;
- root translation, foot skating, lifted footwear, unstable support, or a
  changed facing;
- detached, duplicated, fused, penetrating, or anatomically reversed limbs;
- an invented prop or hand spacing that reads as gripping an invisible object;
- background contamination, fringe pixels, transparency holes inside solid
  body regions, or non-canonical canvas placement.

These twelve poses are authored review material only. This direction does not
grant visual approval, product approval, or runtime admission.
