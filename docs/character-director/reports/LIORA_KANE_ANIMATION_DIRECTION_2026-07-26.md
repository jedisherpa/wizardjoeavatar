# Liora Kane Animation Direction

Date: 2026-07-26
Scope: authored poses `037`-`048`
Candidate state: technical review complete; product approval pending

## Performance Identity

Liora reads as a patient, grounded listener who helps another person retain
agency. Her acting is warm and legible without becoming broad or theatrical.
Motion begins in attention, expands only as far as the conversational beat
requires, and settles before the next idea.

The authored work preserves her long brown curls, warm face, gray hoodie with
drawstrings and pockets, teal plaid trousers, white shoes, voxel construction,
and stable proportions. The blue book exists only in G8. It is one continuous
prop: it may open, turn toward the audience, and close, but it may not
duplicate, disappear, change hands unpredictably, or change color.

## G7 Empathetic Dialogue

Intent: `notice -> invite -> receive -> empathize -> offer -> return agency`

| Pose | Beat | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `037` | Notice | Gentle three-quarter incline with two low open hands. |
| `038` | Invite | One palm rises to offer the conversational floor. |
| `039` | Receive | Head and shoulders soften; both hands draw inward. |
| `040` | Empathize | Right hand rests lightly over the upper chest. |
| `041` | Offer | Two open palms extend at different heights. |
| `042` | Return agency | One palm remains offered while the other settles. |

The sequence runs at 10 fps and holds its final frame. Poses `038`, `040`, and
`042` are intentional holds. Poses `037`, `038`, `040`, and `042` are safe
interruption markers. Entry is from front or three-quarter idle; exit is to
either idle or the G8 book sequence.

## G8 Book Explanation

Intent: `frame -> open -> locate -> realize -> share -> close and retain`

| Pose | Beat | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `043` | Frame | Closed blue book retained at her right side. |
| `044` | Open | One book opens at center chest with both hands supporting it. |
| `045` | Locate | One finger marks a single place on simple pale pages. |
| `046` | Realize | Gaze lifts and the free hand makes a contained insight beat. |
| `047` | Share | Open book and free palm present the useful idea. |
| `048` | Retain | Book closes and returns securely to her right side. |

The sequence runs at 10 fps and holds its final frame. Poses `044`, `045`,
`047`, and `048` are intentional holds. Poses `043`, `044`, `045`, `047`, and
`048` are safe interruption markers. Entry is from idle, front book carry, or
G7; exit is to front idle or front book carry.

## Contact And Rejection Rules

All twelve poses use a fixed root with both shoes planted on canonical baseline
`1185`. Weight may soften or shift inside the footprint, but the shoes may not
slide, lift, cross, or imply locomotion.

Reject cropped curls, missing drawstrings, plaid drift, floating hands,
penetrating limbs, exaggerated waves, command points, alarmed expressions,
idle duplicates, root translation, unstable footwear, or any frame whose book
continuity cannot be read instantly.

These authored poses are review material only. They do not grant product
approval or runtime admission.
